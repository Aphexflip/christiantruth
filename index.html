The debate rules.

The site gets stronger by refusing easy propaganda. A brutal claim with weak evidence ranks below a brutal claim directly stated in the text and supported by strong historical evidence.

1. Attack the propositionThe target is claims, doctrines, texts, institutions, and evidence—not harassment of believers.
2. Steelman before rebuttalUse the strongest defensible version of the religious argument. Beating a weak caricature proves nothing.
3. Rank severity separatelyA 10/10 Damning Score means “high consequence if true,” not “10/10 proof.” Evidence confidence is shown separately.
4. Separate four questionsWhat does the text say? What probably happened historically? What follows philosophically? Is it morally defensible?
5. Link primary evidenceWhere possible, show scripture, papers, museum evidence, university scholarship, or reference works.
6. Correct ourselves publiclyReligious truth claims should be falsifiable; our claims should be too.
