Rules of engagement

Make the argument survive contact.

The atmosphere can be merciless. The evidence cannot be sloppy. Every defense gets its strongest reasonable form before it gets tested.

01 // TARGET THE CLAIMCriticize doctrines, scriptures, institutions, historical actions, and arguments—not people merely for belonging to a religion.
02 // STEELMAN FIRSTUse the strongest defensible Christian reply. If the rebuttal only beats a caricature, it does not belong here.
03 // FOLLOW THE PIVOTWhen the answer changes, track the change. The defense tree exists so arguments cannot quietly switch claims midstream.
04 // SPLIT THE QUESTIONSText, history, philosophy, and morality are separate evidence problems. Do not blur them.
05 // SHOW RECEIPTSPrimary texts, papers, universities, museums, science agencies, and serious scholarship outrank memes and quote graphics.
06 // KILL OUR BAD ARGUMENTSIf a skeptical claim fails, mark it failed. Credibility is more useful than ideological consistency.
