Who benefited from the Bible?

Did biblical texts function as tools of control? The serious answer is more interesting than a conspiracy theory: kings, temples, priests, scribes, and later imperial churches could all gain authority from sacred law — while other biblical voices attacked those same elites.

How this site argues.

The goal is not to insult believers. It is to make weak claims expensive by attaching evidence, context, and sources to them.

1. Attack claims, not peopleA person's religion does not make them stupid or dishonest. The proposition still has to earn belief.
2. Separate fact from inference"The text says X" can be certain while "the author intended Y" may remain debated. We label the difference.
3. Keep the strongest counterargumentIf a skeptical talking point is sloppy, we say so. Bad arguments make the entire case easier to dismiss.
